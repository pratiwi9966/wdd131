IT'S TIME TO VISIT

Taiwan's Outer Island

Discover Taiwan's outer islands—where friendly communities, stunning seascapes, and laid-back island life come together. Relax, explore, and enjoy a world far from the city.

Why Visit Taiwan's Outer Islands?

Atmosphere 👀 Sighteeing

Taiwan's outer islands are the perfect escape for warm weather, clear beaches, and a relaxed island vibe.
Enjoy stunning sights like Penghu's basalt cliffs,
Green Island's saltwater hot springs, and Orchid Island's unique culture and mountain scenery.


Food 😎 best time to visit

Try local favorites such as cactus ice cream, fresh seafood, and traditional flying-fish dishes.
The best time to visit is April-June and September-November, when the weather is sunny and ideal for outdoor exploring.
