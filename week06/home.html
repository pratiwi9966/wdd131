Taiwan's Outer Island

Discover Taiwan's outer islands—where friendly communities, stunning seascapes, and laid-back island life come together. Relax, explore, and enjoy a world far from the city.

Why Visit Taiwan's Outer Islands?

Atmosphere 👀 Sighteeing

Taiwan's outer islands are the perfect escape for warm weather, clear beaches, and a relaxed island vibe.
Enjoy stunning sights like Penghu's basalt cliffs,
Green Island's saltwater hot springs, and Orchid Island's unique culture and mountain scenery.


Best time to visit

Orchid Island (Lanyu)

April to June — During late spring, weather tends to be pleasantly warm and stable (around 24-30 °C / 75-86 °F), and you avoid the worst of summer's typhoons. This is also close to the start of the Flying Fish Festival season, a meaningful cultural experience tied to local Indigenous traditions.


Penghu Island

Spring (March-May) — Pleasant, mild temperatures (about 20-25 °C), comfortable sea and sky, perfect for island-hopping, beach days, and enjoying scenic views. Ferry and boat services are usually reliable.
